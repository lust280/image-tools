Foto is a small set of image utilities for people who do not want to hand photos to a server. Open a tool, pick a file, and save the result. You can add this page to your home screen if you use it often.

## Why this stays on your device

There is no account and no upload step. Conversion, compression, resizing, and cropping run in the browser you already have open. A batch of photos can be saved one by one, or together as a ZIP.

## FAQ

### Is Foto free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use.

### Do you store my images?

No. Files you pick are processed in this tab. They are not sent to Foto or to the host. When you close the tab, that working copy is gone. The download stays wherever you saved it.

### Which browsers work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, including phones. HEIC photos open directly in Safari. Other browsers download a decoder that is part of this site and still run it locally.

### Can I put Foto on my home screen?

Yes. In Safari or Chrome, use the share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like a small site, without an app-store install.
