PNG is a good format when you need transparency or sharp edges. JPG is what many forms, email apps, and older tools expect, and it is usually smaller for photos. This page re-encodes PNG files to JPG locally.

## How to convert PNG to JPG

1. Choose one or more PNG files.
2. Set JPEG quality. 90 is a strong default for photos.
3. Download the JPG, or a ZIP if you converted more than one.

Transparent pixels become white, because JPG has no transparency. If you need to keep the transparent areas, use [PNG and JPG to WebP](tool:png-jpg-to-webp) or stay with PNG.

## FAQ

### Are the files uploaded?

No. The browser reads the PNG and writes the JPG on this device.

### Why is the background white?

JPG cannot store transparency. Foto fills those pixels with white so you do not get a black box in apps that ignore alpha.

### Will a screenshot get blurry?

Flat colors and text survive better as PNG. Use a high quality setting, or keep the PNG if the file is a diagram or interface shot.

### Can I convert several PNGs together?

Yes. Add a batch and download a ZIP.
