WebP is common on the web and awkward in some editors, slides, and print tools. This page turns it into JPG or PNG without uploading the file. Choose PNG to keep transparency. Choose JPG for a smaller photo, and transparent pixels will be filled with white.

## How to convert WebP

1. Choose one or more `.webp` files.
2. Pick JPG or PNG. The quality slider applies to JPG.
3. Download the result, or a ZIP for several files.

## FAQ

### Which format should I pick?

Use JPG for photos you will email or drop into a doc. Use PNG when the WebP has a transparent background, such as a logo or sticker.

### Are WebP files uploaded?

No. The browser decodes and re-encodes them locally.

### Why is my PNG larger?

PNG is lossless. A photographic WebP turned into PNG often grows. That is expected.

### Can I convert a batch?

Yes. Add several WebP files and download a ZIP. Every file uses the format you selected.
