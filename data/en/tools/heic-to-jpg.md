HEIC is the default photo format on recent iPhones. It keeps detail in a small file, and a lot of websites, editors, and older phones still ask for JPG. This page turns HEIC and HEIF into JPG on the device you are holding.

## How to convert HEIC to JPG

1. Tap the box and choose one or more `.heic` photos. On a computer you can drop them in.
2. Leave quality at 90, or lower it when you need a smaller file.
3. Save each JPG, or download a ZIP when you converted several.

## FAQ

### Are my photos uploaded?

No. Decoding and JPG encoding run in this browser tab. The only download is the decoder that belongs to this website, and it runs locally. See [Privacy](site:privacy).

### Why might my iPhone hand over a JPEG already?

Safari sometimes converts HEIC when you pick a photo. You can still save that JPEG here, and it is still not uploaded. This page asks for `.heic` and `.heif` files so the original can come through when the browser allows it.

### Will the picture look worse?

JPG is lossy. At quality 90 the change is hard to see on a phone screen. Move the slider down only when file size matters more than fine detail.

### Can I convert a batch?

Yes. Add several photos and use Download ZIP. They are converted one after another so a long batch on a phone can take a moment.

### What if a file fails?

A few HEIC variants cannot be decoded in every browser. Try the same file in Safari, or export a JPG from the Photos app. The original stays where it was.
