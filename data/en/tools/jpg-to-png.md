Some editors, printers, and upload forms only accept PNG. This page wraps your JPG as a PNG on this device. PNG is lossless, so the file often grows. It does not recover detail the original JPEG already discarded.

## How to convert JPG to PNG

1. Choose one or more `.jpg` or `.jpeg` files.
2. Wait for the preview. There is no quality slider, because PNG does not use JPEG quality.
3. Download the PNG, or a ZIP for a batch.

## FAQ

### Why is the PNG bigger than the JPG?

JPG throws away information to stay small. PNG stores the pixels it is given. Converting cannot invent the detail the JPEG removed, and the new file is often larger.

### Is this private?

Yes. The picture is not uploaded. Read the [privacy notes](site:privacy).

### Should I use WebP instead?

If the place you are sending the image accepts WebP, [PNG and JPG to WebP](tool:png-jpg-to-webp) is usually smaller than PNG.

### Can I convert a folder of photos?

Add up to 24 files and download them as one ZIP.
