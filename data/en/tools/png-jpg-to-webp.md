WebP is widely supported in current browsers and many site builders, and it is often smaller than JPG or PNG. This converter runs locally. A PNG with transparency stays transparent. A JPG is re-encoded at the quality you pick.

## How to convert to WebP

1. Choose PNG or JPG files.
2. Set quality. 80 is a practical start for photos.
3. Download the WebP files, or a ZIP.

## FAQ

### Does WebP keep transparency?

Yes when the source is a PNG with transparent pixels. JPG has no transparency to keep.

### Will every website accept WebP?

Most modern ones will. If a form still rejects it, convert back with [WebP to JPG and PNG](tool:webp-to-jpg-png).

### Is the file uploaded?

No. Encoding happens in this browser.

### Why did the file not get smaller?

A PNG of a screenshot can already be efficient, and a very high quality setting keeps more data. Lower the slider and compare the size shown on the result.
