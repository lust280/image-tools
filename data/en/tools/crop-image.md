Social apps crop for you if the shape is wrong, and they rarely crop where you hoped. This page uses the sizes people actually upload:

- Instagram post, 1080×1080
- Instagram portrait, 1080×1350
- Instagram story, 1080×1920
- YouTube thumbnail, 1280×720
- Facebook post, 1200×630
- X post, 1600×900
- LinkedIn post, 1200×627
- Pinterest pin, 1000×1500
- TikTok, 1080×1920

The frame starts centered. Drag it, or drag a corner, until the subject sits where you want it.

## How to crop

1. Choose a photo. Add more if you want the same preset on each, then tap a thumbnail to adjust that one.
2. Pick the network preset. Changing the preset recenters the frame.
3. Tap Crop, then save the file or a ZIP.

## FAQ

### Which preset is the YouTube thumbnail?

The 16:9 preset labeled YouTube thumbnail. It exports 1280×720.

### Can I crop several photos?

Yes. Each image keeps its own frame. Create them all and download a ZIP, or save one at a time.

### Does the crop get uploaded?

No. The preview and the exported file are made in this browser.

### What if my photo is smaller than the preset?

Foto still exports the preset’s pixel size, which upscales a small source. For a sharper result, start with a photo at least as large as the preset, or [resize](tool:image-resizer) only when you do not need those exact dimensions.
